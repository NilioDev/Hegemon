# Computer players

Watched on fan patch 1.51 under DOSBox, average difficulty. The game was
the Alkari's 4-player game from stardate 3500.1, and the original saved it
every turn for 23 turns. Its saved games were read with Hegemon's reader.
What the computer players do comes from their own rules inside the
original, which only their moves show. Hegemon copies the moves it saw;
everything else is marked as assumed.

## When

Hegemon's computer players take their turn when the human ends theirs,
before the ships move (assumed). A colony ship that reaches its star
settles in the same turn: the colony shows up in the save made after that
turn (seen).

## Research

At the start each computer player had a field: Optronics for the Klackons,
Fusion Physics for the Meklars and the Bulrathi. When a field was done they
chose the cheapest one they could research: Advanced Engineering (80 RP)
for the Meklars, Astro Biology (80 RP) for the Klackons. Hegemon picks the
cheapest field, and at random between fields of the same cost (assumed).

## Colonies

- **Home colonies:** for 23 turns, most of them made trade goods. The
  Klackons also made transports and freighters (not copied yet).
- **New colonies of one person:** they made housing, or marine barracks
  that never finished. Hegemon gives them housing.
- **Jobs:** the Meklars' home went from 7 farmers and a worker to 2
  farmers, 2 workers and 4 scientists in the next turn. Homes that already
  had scientists were left as they were. Hegemon sets a colony of two or
  more people without scientists the way it sets up a new colony: as few
  farmers as feed them, then scientists and workers (assumed).

## Colony ships

Each computer player's colony ship went to the nearest star with a planet
to settle, never to a star of its own (the planets of the home systems were
left).

At that star it took a planet whose climate can be farmed over toxic,
radiated or barren ones, then the planet that holds the most people:

- the Klackons a small terran planet (8 people) over a large desert one;
- the Meklars a huge radiated planet (6) over a large barren one (5);
- the Bulrathi a small tundra planet (3) over a medium barren one (4).

Hegemon picks the same planets, and settles them in the same turn.

## Scouts

The scouts went out to stars that nobody of their player had seen and no
other ship was going to. When they got there, they came back to one of
their player's colonies, then went out again. Hegemon sends each scout at
a colony to the nearest such star, and a scout elsewhere to the nearest of
its player's colony stars. The original sent the second scout further than
the nearest star (not copied yet). The colony ships choose before the
scouts, so the scouts go elsewhere.

## Not copied yet

- Money: the original's computer players had about 60 BC more after the
  second turn than their income explains.
- The ships they build, outposts, warships, combat, diplomacy, and how the
  difficulty changes what they do.
